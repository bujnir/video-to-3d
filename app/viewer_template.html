Smooth surface

Smooth it into a surface

For measuring and route planning the photo look gets in the way, so we turned the splat into one smooth, solid surface. Depth maps rendered from all 104 camera positions are fused into a single volume, the surface is pulled out of it and then smoothed. What is left is pure shape, coloured by height like a topographic map, with a contour line at every height step.

Method: depth fusion (TSDF) + marching cubes + Taubin smoothing, on CPU · – triangles · – min

Shapes (SketchUp style)

Simplify it into shapes

For layouts, clearances and flight planning you want what an architect draws in SketchUp: a flat floor, straight walls and a simple box for each piece of furniture. We get that automatically from the surface: the floor is fitted as a plane, the walls as a rectangle around it, and every object becomes one or more boxes, split into a low and a high part where needed (a sofa's seat and back). An object with floor visible underneath becomes a table: a top on four legs.

Method: plane fit + top-down height map + box fitting, on CPU in seconds · – boxes · – objects
